Questions

Is it free?

Yes, for personal and other noncommercial use. Selling it or using it in a paid product needs the author's permission.

Does it send any data?

No. There is no account, no tracking and no internet connection. Your audio is never saved or sent anywhere.

How many Bluetooth devices can play at once?

That depends on your PC's Bluetooth adapter, usually two to four. Audora has no limit of its own.

Why can't I change the sound of my default device?

Windows plays that device directly. Turn on Full control in Settings, with a free virtual audio cable, and every device can be changed.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings, Apps, Audora, Uninstall. For the portable version, delete the folder.
