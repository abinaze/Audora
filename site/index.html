Everything in one simple window

Many devices at once

Play the same sound on as many devices as your PC can handle, in any mix of Bluetooth, USB, wired and HDMI.

Volume for each device

Every device has its own volume (up to 200%), mute, left/right balance and delay to line up slower devices like Bluetooth.

Master volume that matches Windows

One slider that moves with the Windows volume bar and your keyboard volume keys, and controls all your devices together.

Sound profiles

Music, Movie, Communication and Gaming, plus Smart, which picks the right one for the app you are using.

Groups

Save the devices you use together, like Living room or Headphones only, and play them in one click with their own volume.

Bluetooth, handled

See your paired Bluetooth speakers and headphones and connect them from inside Audora. It brings them back by itself when they return.

Private by design

No account, no tracking and no internet connection needed. Your audio is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Lives in your tray

Close the window and the music keeps playing. Quick controls are one right-click away. Optionally starts with Windows.

How it works

  1. 1

    Listen

    Audora listens to what your Windows default device is playing.

  2. 2

    Copy

    It plays the same sound on the other devices you switch on.

  3. 3

    Control

    You set volume, sound and delay for each device, or for a whole group.

Windows plays the default device directly, so the sound effects work on the other devices. To use them on every device, turn on Full control in Settings (it uses a free virtual audio cable).

Works with the audio devices you already have

  • Bluetooth headphones and earbuds
  • Bluetooth speakers
  • USB headphones and speakers
  • USB audio adapters
  • 3.5 mm wired headphones
  • HDMI and DisplayPort screens and TVs
  • Anything else Windows can play sound on

A sound for what you are doing

Music

Fuller bass, clearer highs and a slightly wider sound.

Movie

Deeper bass, clearer dialogue and wide surround. Evens out loud scenes and quiet speech.

Communication

Clear, centred voices for calls. Cuts low rumble and evens out the volume.

Gaming

Sharper footsteps and directions, with a wide, positional sound.

Smart picks one of these for you. It looks at the name of the app in front, on your PC only, and never stores or sends it. Plus a 10-band equalizer and your own saved presets.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit
  • No extra software needed
  • A Bluetooth adapter if you want to use Bluetooth devices

Install in three steps

  1. Download and open Audora-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says "unknown publisher", click More info, then Run anyway. The installer is not code-signed yet.
  3. Open Audora, start some music and switch on the devices you want.

Every release comes with a checksum file so you can check your download. See the install guide.

Questions

Is Audora free?

Yes, for personal and other noncommercial use, under the PolyForm Noncommercial License. Selling Audora or using it in a paid product needs the author's permission.

Does it send any data?

No. There is no account, no tracking and no internet connection. Audio is read in memory only to play it on your other devices, and is never saved or sent.

How many Bluetooth devices can play at once?

That depends on your PC's Bluetooth adapter, usually two to four. Audora has no limit of its own.

Why can't I change the sound of my default device?

Windows plays that device directly. Turn on Full control in Settings, with a free virtual audio cable, and Audora plays every device itself.

Why does Windows warn me when I install?

The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows does not know the publisher. Choose More info, then Run anyway. You can check the download with the checksum file.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings, Apps, Audora, Uninstall. It asks whether to delete your settings too. For the portable version, delete the folder.
