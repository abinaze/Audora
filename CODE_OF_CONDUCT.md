# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want Audora to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever your
experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback on ideas and code, not on people.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you disagree.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of harassment, insults or personal attacks.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination or hateful language
- Threats, doxxing or sharing someone's private information
- Repeated disruption of discussions after being asked to stop

## Reporting a problem

Report it privately: open **Security > Report a vulnerability** on the repository and say
it is a conduct report, or contact the maintainer ([@abinaze](https://github.com/abinaze))
through GitHub. Reports are handled privately and taken seriously.

## What happens next

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these
rules. We will try to be fair and to explain our decisions.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
